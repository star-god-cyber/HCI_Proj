Relevance
RUBRIC DEFINITION & CRITERIA

Relevance & Directness

What This Metric Measures:
Measures whether the candidate addresses the exact question prompt directly without drifting into unprompted filler context.

★ Exceeds Bar (8.8–10.0): Directly addresses core situation in opening sentence; tightly aligned with role and question objectives.
✓ Meets Bar (7.3–8.7): Partially answers prompt but includes minor tangentials or generic preamble before reaching the core point.
⚠️ Below Bar (<7.3): Misunderstands or evades prompt; gives generic theoretical lecture without answering the actual scenario.
8.0/10

Situation is clearly framed.

Specificity
RUBRIC DEFINITION & CRITERIA

Specificity & Technical Depth

What This Metric Measures:
Evaluates concrete terminology, named tools, technologies, system architectures, and precise operational steps rather than broad generalities.

★ Exceeds Bar (8.8–10.0): Names exact tools, technologies, trade-offs, architectures, and concrete steps with senior-level depth.
✓ Meets Bar (7.3–8.7): Mentions broad technical steps and generic tools with moderate clarity.
⚠️ Below Bar (<7.3): Speaks only in high-level platitudes ('we collaborated', 'we solved it') without technical depth or concrete artifacts.
6.0/10

Mentions basic steps.

Evidence & Outcome
RUBRIC DEFINITION & CRITERIA

Evidence & Outcome (STAR Result)

What This Metric Measures:
Verifies the presence of verifiable business impact, quantifiable metrics (latency, GMV, conversion %, test coverage), and clear resolution deliverables.

★ Exceeds Bar (8.8–10.0): Explicitly states concrete quantitative metrics, KPIs, project completion timelines, and permanent organizational impact.
✓ Meets Bar (7.3–8.7): Describes positive resolution, but metrics are vague or qualitative rather than quantified with numbers/percentages.
⚠️ Below Bar (<7.3): Stops entirely at the action taken; completely omits what occurred afterwards or whether the problem was resolved.
4.0/10

Missing tangible outcome/metric.

Structure & Clarity
RUBRIC DEFINITION & CRITERIA

Structure & Narrative Arc (STAR)

What This Metric Measures:
Assesses coherent chronological progression following Situation, Task, Action, and Result with clear transitions and signposting.

★ Exceeds Bar (8.8–10.0): Flawless STAR progression with clear chronological signposting and concise transitions between problem and impact.
✓ Meets Bar (7.3–8.7): Follows chronological order, but transitions between action and result are slightly blurred or jumpy.
⚠️ Below Bar (<7.3): Disorganized, rambling, or fragmented narrative without clear beginning, middle, or resolution.
6.0/10

Actions outlined sequentially.
